Privacy Policy for OffChat

Last updated: November 11, 2026

MH Innovations ("we," "our," or "us") operates the OffChat mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

OffChat is designed as an offline-first mesh messaging application. We do not operate servers that collect or store your personal information or message content. All direct communications are transmitted peer-to-peer between devices.

a. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device to provide its core functionality. This data is never transmitted to us or any third party and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • Your chosen display name and profile picture.
  • Your message history, including text, photos, documents, and voice notes.
  • App settings and preferences (e.g., chat backgrounds, notification settings).

b. Data Collected by Third-Party Services (AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain information to serve personalized ads, including:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier).
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Device information (model, operating system version).
  • App interaction data (e.g., ad views and clicks).

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy policy. You can learn more here: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's ad settings.

c. Device Permissions

To enable its offline mesh features, the App requests the following permissions, which are used solely for their stated purposes:

  • Nearby Devices (Bluetooth & Wi-Fi): Required to discover and connect directly with other OffChat users nearby.
  • Microphone: Used only when you choose to record a voice message.
  • Location (Optional): Used only when you choose to share your location with a contact. It is not collected in the background.
  • Storage/Photos: Used only when you choose to send or save a photo or document.

2. How We Use Your Information

We do not collect or use your personal data. The limited data collected by our third-party partner, AdMob, is used solely to display advertisements within the App.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information. The only data sharing that occurs is with Google AdMob, as described in Section 1b.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You have full control over it. You can delete your chat history at any time from within the App. Uninstalling the App will permanently remove all locally stored data. If you have any questions about data deletion, you can contact us.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. All data is stored locally, and we do not operate servers that collect user data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
